How we work

A pipeline stays secure when the people along it agree it should. Our process builds that agreement, then protects it.

  1. Read the corridor

    We study the terrain, the seasons and the stakeholders before we propose anything.

  2. Agree with the community

    We sit down with host communities and leaders to agree how the corridor will be shared and protected.

  3. Monitor and respond

    We combine field intelligence with digital monitoring to spot problems early.

  4. Review and renew

    We report openly, review the agreement regularly and adjust as conditions change.

Smart Petroleum Governance

Africa cannot secure, measure or fairly distribute what it cannot see.

Modernisation

Digital monitoring that watches a line in real time, and security infrastructure that anticipates trouble instead of reacting to it.

Transparency

Data-driven decision frameworks that replace rumour with record, so every stakeholder can see what is happening.

Efficiency

Less loss to theft, leakage and delay, so more of the value of every barrel reaches the people who live beside it.

Tejere Jolomi Thomas

Chief Executive Officer, Tejere Anderson Services

Tejere has spent more than two decades in oil and gas, across upstream operations, offshore and exploration, pipeline security and community risk management. His strength is upstream work and the development of community programmes that bring host communities in as partners. He knows the onshore, the swamp and the creek first-hand.

He is also Executive Vice President of Ocean Wave Group, where since 2024 he has led the protection of pipeline networks and the coordination of stakeholders along complex energy corridors. An indigene of Kokori in Delta State, he is known as a trusted mediator between investors, government, contractors and host communities.
