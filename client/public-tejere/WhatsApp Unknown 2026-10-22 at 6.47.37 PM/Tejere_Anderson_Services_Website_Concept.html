The upstream lifecycle, with the community at each stage

Every stage of an upstream project touches the people who live nearby. We plan the technical work and the community programme together.

  1. Survey and explore

    Agree access and expectations with host communities before survey crews arrive.

  2. Drill and appraise

    Keep neighbours informed while wells are drilled, so the site stays welcome.

  3. Develop the field

    Build community programmes alongside the development plan, not after it.

  4. Produce and sustain

    Review programmes regularly and report openly as production runs.

Tejere Jolomi Thomas

Chief Executive Officer, Tejere Anderson Services

Tejere has more than 20 years of experience in upstream operations, deep offshore and exploration, and the development of community programmes and engagement. He has worked across onshore, swamp and creek environments in the Niger Delta.

His focus is upstream operations that involve host communities as partners from the start. An indigene of Kokori, Ethiope East Local Government Area, Delta State, he works between energy investors, government institutions, contractors and host communities.

He is also Executive Vice President of Ocean Wave Group, where since 2024 he has led pipeline network protection.
